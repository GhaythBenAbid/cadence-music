# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss code, evidence and product behavior; avoid personal attacks. Harassment, discrimination, threats and sharing another person's private information are unacceptable.

Maintain clear, constructive discussions. Maintainers may edit/remove harmful content, close disruptive threads or restrict participation to protect the community. Apply these expectations to issues, reviews and other project spaces.

Use GitHub's reporting tools for abusive platform conduct. Report security-sensitive matters through the private route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a public issue. Do not publish private details to report a concern. Ordinary bugs and feature proposals belong in the issue templates.
